Ambiguity Resolution for Text-to-SQL

Cheol Kim, with a first-year Ph.D. student — DIAL Lab, SKKU

Manuscript prepared for VLDB 2026 Not submitted

A training-free method for natural-language questions that admit several valid SQL readings. Candidate interpretations are enumerated, pruned against database contents, grounded where only one reading survives, and expanded only over the spans that remain underspecified — improving recall by 11.2% over training-free zero-shot baselines on AMBIQT while returning about five candidate queries.

Text-to-SQL for Manufacturing Databases

Industry–university collaboration — DIAL Lab, SKKU

2025 – 2026

A natural-language interface for querying a client production database. Built a 5,000-pair question–SQL dataset with a graduate student, combining programmatic generation with LLM paraphrasing and manual validation. Proposed diversity and coverage metrics over question embeddings to detect and correct sampling bias. Benchmarked Qwen2.5, Llama and Codestral, and delivered a model-agnostic few-shot pipeline so the client could substitute commercial APIs without retraining.
