Ambiguity resolution for Text-to-SQL

DIAL Lab2026with a first-year Ph.D. student

A training-free method for questions that have several valid SQL readings. Rather than committing to one interpretation, it enumerates candidates, prunes them against what is actually in the database, and expands only the spans that remain genuinely underspecified — returning about five candidate queries instead of one guess.
